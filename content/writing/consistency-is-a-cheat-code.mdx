One of the ideas that came out of this was Health Score: making healthy actions and consistency measurable, so progress is visible before it shows up anywhere else.

## Building for the hard days

The lesson I keep coming back to is that a health product isn't really competing with other apps. It's competing with the day someone is having. Build for that day.
